Finance Hub helps its owner and personally invited users collect financial emails and preserve receipts and statements for bookkeeping, budgeting, and tax preparation.

Collect originals

Read matching emails from an authorized Gmail account, including available attachments, and preserve the original records.

Keep them organized

Store originals in designated Google Drive folders. Save separate processing checkpoints so collection can resume between runs.

Prepare for review

Extract supported transaction details as candidates for review. An optional export writes transaction candidates and a review list to a designated Google Sheet. Business and personal classification and budgets are planned next steps.

What connecting an account means

The current collector requests read-only Gmail access and broad Google Drive access. Drive authorization is account-wide, although the collector is configured to work with designated archive and system-state folders. It reads and creates files there; it retains the newest 20 recovery snapshots and can move older snapshots to Drive Trash after a successful restore check. Originals and shared recovery files are retained, and sharing permissions are unchanged.

Collection runs on the owner's computer or in GitHub Actions. This website is informational: it has no sign-in form, financial dashboard, or access to your mailbox.

Read the privacy policy for details about data access, storage, retention, and revoking access.
